03 / LAB NOTES

Network detective.

01

My connection

My ISP is UNITEL LLC, also known as Beeline Uzbekistan (AS41202). My default gateway is an iPhone personal hotspot.

02

A small hardware mystery

The hotspot’s MAC address is locally administered, so maclookup.app could not identify a maker.

03

The route to Inha

Traceroute got answers from 7 hops on the way to inha.uz, then the routers stopped answering. Replies from inha.uz arrive with TTL 57. Starting from TTL 64, that means 64 − 57 = 7 routers, so the server is about 8 hops away.

04

Distance takes time

Connecting to inha.uz took about 0.04 seconds. Connecting to the University of Tokyo took about 1.3 seconds. Signals take time to travel farther, and the longer path through more networks also adds delay.

Names, packets, and privacy
